<h2 align="center">About Me</h2>
<p>
  Hello, I'm Edileudo Maciel, just a guy who's absolutely passionate about learning and has way too many interests.

I'm currently a Computer Science student at Universidade Federal do Ceará, in Brazil, just starting my career in tech. I'm always coming up with new ideas and wishing I could somehow make them real, things I could actually experience, use, and see happening. I guess programming became the way I found to bring at least some of the things that show up in my mind to life.

So here, I'm going to share everything from small university projects to more ambitious and meaningful work I may build in the future.

I hope I can eventually share with the world some of what I have to offer, solve problems creatively and effectively, create things that might inspire or help someone someday, and use this space as a way to express myself through the things I build.
